# InsightFlow AI — Product Overview

InsightFlow AI is a fictional B2B SaaS product created for a portfolio RAG project. It helps sales, pre-sales, product operations, and customer success teams turn static product documents and business data into searchable AI-assisted workflows.

## Core Modules

### AI Customer Segmentation Dashboard
- Connects to CSV, MySQL, PostgreSQL, or BI-exported data.
- Generates customer segments based on user behavior, spending, purchase frequency, and engagement.
- Produces AI-assisted recommendations with human-in-the-loop review.

### AI Pre-sales Knowledge Assistant
- Converts FAQ, product documentation, pricing notes, deployment guides, security documents, and customer cases into a searchable RAG knowledge base.
- Retrieves relevant context when a user asks a customer-facing question.
- Generates direct answers, email drafts, solution summaries, objection-handling responses, and source references.

## Target Users
- AI pre-sales interns
- Sales engineers
- Solution consultants
- Customer success teams
- Product operations teams
- International business development teams

## What It Does Not Do
InsightFlow AI does not train foundation models from scratch. It focuses on LLM application workflows: retrieval, answer generation, business recommendation drafting, and human review.
