# InsightFlow AI — Customer Case Studies

## Case Study 1: E-commerce Customer Segmentation
A mid-sized e-commerce company had dashboards showing customer behavior, but business users still needed to manually interpret charts and convert insights into campaigns.

InsightFlow AI processed customer behavior data, generated customer segments, calculated weighted AOV, and produced AI-generated marketing recommendations with review status.

## Case Study 2: SaaS Pre-sales Knowledge Assistant
A SaaS company received repeated questions about deployment, pricing, integrations, and security.

InsightFlow AI converted product FAQ, deployment guides, pricing notes, and security documentation into a RAG knowledge base. Sales users could ask natural language questions and receive customer-ready answers with source references.

## Case Study 3: Cross-border Product Support
A cross-border commerce team needed consistent English responses about product, logistics, and return policies.

InsightFlow AI built a multilingual knowledge assistant based on policy documents, logistics FAQs, and product descriptions. The team generated English response drafts faster while keeping answers aligned with internal policies.
