# InsightFlow AI — Pre-sales Objection Handling

## Objection: We already have a FAQ page. Why do we need an AI assistant?
A FAQ page requires users to search manually. An AI assistant lets users ask natural language questions, retrieves relevant FAQ or documentation content, and generates a customer-ready response.

## Objection: We are worried that AI may hallucinate.
That is valid. The recommended design uses RAG, source references, and human review. The assistant retrieves source content first and generates answers based on that content.

## Objection: Can this work with our existing documents?
Yes. The system can start with existing FAQ, deployment guides, pricing notes, security documents, and case studies.

## Objection: Is this only useful for customer support?
No. Pre-sales teams can use it to answer product questions, explain integrations, prepare proposal content, handle objections, and draft follow-up emails.

## Objection: We do not want AI to send messages automatically.
The system can generate draft answers only. Human approval is required before customer communication.

## Objection: How do we measure success?
Common metrics include answer accuracy, source relevance, time saved, human approval rate, unresolved question rate, and reduction in repeated internal search requests.
